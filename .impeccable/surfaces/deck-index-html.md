# Surface brief: workshop deck

Scope: `deck/` (index.html, theme.css, slides.md, images/kot). Mode: Read (projected talk; the room understands an argument). Audience and constraints live in PRODUCT.md. Projected live only, 1280x720, speaker notes carry detail; on-screen copy trimmed, every cut listed for the presenter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every slide is one kitchen order ticket, the intent handed to the kitchen, because "an agent will cook". Refuses the conference default of boxed tiles, eyebrow labels and grid-paper chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Thermal paper #fbfaf6 ground, thermal ink #1f1e24, a five-step ink ramp only. Human = red thermal ink #c4372a; agent = reverse-printed black bar with paper text. Archivo condensed 800 for headlines, Archivo 400 for lines, Spline Sans Mono only for the ticket header, times, code. Dashed tear rules are the only rules. Two type sizes per slide. Images are 1-bit dithered prints numbered FIG. 01–06.

STORY: The room reads one order at a time, hears the rest from the speaker, and always knows where it is: order number, section, clock.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running ticket header across the top (ORDER #001 left, section centre, IST time right) over one dashed tear rule; headline at ~100px condensed, left, in the upper third; empty paper below; perforated bottom edge.

FORM: Kitchen Order Ticket, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:654db2bac25c644a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises: one-size discipline (cutting bench); strict ink ramp (exposure record); /btw as a second ticket clipped beneath (sticker accretion); memes as numbered plates (curved crease); section openers as mostly blank stock (cloud quarry).
